Research

I am an applied mathematician specializing in stochastic modeling and uncertainty quantification. My research bridges theoretical mathematics and practical computation, focusing on methodological tools that combine rigorous theory, advanced numerical methods, high-performance computing, and physical interpretation. Since my PhD, I have concentrated on stochastic approaches to geophysical fluid dynamics, with a particular emphasis on the development of reliable ensemble forecasting systems.
